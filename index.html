Medhini Narasimhan

Staff Research Scientist · Google DeepMind

Hi! I'm a Staff Research Scientist at Google DeepMind. I currently lead Evals for Veo, our video generation model. In the past, I've worked on Veo's scaling laws, data mixture recipes, image-to-video and other control capabilities, and also post-training. My research interests are in building multimodal models for understanding and generation.

I graduated with a PhD in Computer Science from UC Berkeley in 2023, where I was advised by Prof. Trevor Darrell and was a member of BAIR. Prior to Berkeley, I completed my Master's at UIUC advised by Prof. A. Schwing and Prof. S. Lazebnik, and obtained my Bachelor's from NITK, India.

I am honored to be a Snap Research Scholar (2019) and a Siebel Scholar (2018).
